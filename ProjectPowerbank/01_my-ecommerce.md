## Problem Statement
Outdoor Enthusiasts & Travelers: Frequent use of GPS, cameras, and social media during trips leads to rapid battery depletion, causing users to miss important moments or lose navigation.

Remote Workers & Digital Nomads: Working from cafes or public spaces without accessible power outlets creates a constant struggle to keep laptops and smartphones charged.

Commuters: Users spend hours traveling and need reliable, lightweight power sources that won't add unnecessary bulk to their daily carry.

## Solution(s)
Lifestyle-Based Filtering: Users can browse products by their usage, such as "Travel Ready" (High capacity) or "Work Anywhere" (Laptop charging).

Smart Charge Estimator: An interactive tool that shows exactly how many full charges a power bank provides for specific phone or laptop models.

Safety Guaranteed: All products feature verified safety certifications (TIS/มอก., CE, FCC) to ensure user confidence and device protection.
